How people submit to the inbox
  • Club signup ↗

    “Join the club” in the website menu opens the registration form. Entries appear under Club signups.

  • The AI Review subscription ↗

    Subscribe links in the menu, The AI Review, and the signup page open the email form. Entries appear under The AI Review subscription.

  • Submit to The AI Review ↗

    “Contribute an article,” “Start a draft,” and “Develop an article of your own” open the contribution form. Text and attachments appear under AI Review submissions for an editor to review.

  • Event RSVP ↗

    Select an upcoming or potential event and click RSVP. The popup asks for name, email, and any questions defined for that event. Basic details appear under Event RSVPs (upcoming only); full answers appear in Surveys.

  • Request a workshop ↗

    “Request a workshop” on Events opens a popup form. Entries appear under Workshop requests.

  • Ask about an event ↗

    Select an event, then “Ask about this event.” The popup sends a question linked to that event to Questions.

  • Ask the club ↗

    “Ask the club” in the public website footer opens the general question popup. Entries appear under Questions.

Successful submissions open a confirmation screen with a Close or Done button. Use the sender’s email link in the inbox to reply. Subscription requests are saved for future updates; email broadcasts are not enabled.

Other website actions: game scores go to Rankings ↗. Teams links open the club community. Saving a writing draft keeps it on the visitor’s device; it does not submit an inbox entry.

What do the inbox statuses mean?

Received in club inbox means the submission is stored in the club’s Neon database, not just on this device.

New: waiting for an officer to review. Reviewed: an officer has looked at it; follow-up may still be needed. Archived: no further action is planned.

Mark reviewed and Archive submission move entries between these sections. Neither sends a reply, deletes a record, unsubscribes anyone, or publishes a contribution. You can change the status later. Use the sender’s email link to reply.

New counts update automatically. Browser alerts work while this office tab is open. Email alerts are not connected.
