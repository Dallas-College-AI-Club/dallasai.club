    Help

    How people reach the inbox

    • Club signup ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      “Join the club” in the website menu opens the registration form. Entries appear under Signups.

    • The AI Review subscription ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      Subscribe links in the menu, The AI Review, and the signup page open the email form. Entries appear under The AI Review.

    • Submit to The AI Review ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      “Contribute an article,” “Start a draft,” and “Develop an article of your own” open the contribution form. Text and attachments appear under Articles for an editor to review.

    • Event RSVP ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      Select an upcoming or potential event and click RSVP. The popup asks for name, email, and any questions defined for that event. Basic details appear under RSVPs; full answers appear in Surveys.

    • Request a workshop ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      “Request a workshop” on Events opens a popup form. Entries appear under Workshops.

    • Ask about an event ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      Select an event, then “Ask about this event.” The popup sends a question linked to that event to Questions.

    • Ask the club ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      “Ask the club” in the public website footer opens the general question popup. Entries appear under Questions.

    Successful submissions open a confirmation screen with a Close or Done button. Use the sender’s email link in the inbox to reply. Subscription requests are saved for future updates; email broadcasts are not enabled.

    Other website actions: game scores go to Rankings ↗ (opens in a new tab). Teams links open the club community. Saving a writing draft keeps it on the visitor’s device; it does not submit an inbox entry.

    What New, Reviewed and Archived mean

    Every submission is stored in the club’s Neon database, not just on this device.

    New: waiting for an officer to review. Reviewed: an officer has looked at it; follow-up may still be needed. Archived: no further action is planned. Comments and history are kept, and you can mark an archived submission new or reviewed again.

    Mark reviewed and Archive move submissions between these views. Neither sends a reply, deletes a record, unsubscribes anyone, or publishes a contribution. Use the sender’s email link to reply.

    Sessions

    You stay signed in for 3 days on this browser. If your session ends while you work, Club Office pauses and asks you to sign in again; your unsaved work stays on the page.

    Exports

    Export filtered CSV downloads the submissions that match the current status, type and event. Narrow the filters to 10,000 matching records or fewer first. Every export is logged.

    New submissions and browser alerts

    While Club Office is open, it checks for new submissions every minute and when you return to the tab. The list never changes by itself: a “new submissions” button appears instead, and the Inbox count updates. Turn on Browser alerts in the account menu for pop-ups while this tab is open. Email alerts aren’t available.
