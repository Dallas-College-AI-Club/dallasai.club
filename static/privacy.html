How we use your information

Our forms collect the information you provide: your name and email address, campus and interests for membership, event choices, workshop requests, and contributions or attachments.

Authorized club officers use this information to manage memberships, events, requests, and editorial submissions. We store form records in Neon, process requests through Vercel, store uploaded attachments privately in Vercel Blob, and use Resend to deliver email. Please do not submit student identification numbers, passwords, or sensitive personal documents.

Newsletter subscription is a separate choice. Joining the club or registering for an event does not subscribe you to The AI Review. Newsletter emails include an unsubscribe option, and RSVP emails include a cancellation link.

We use temporary request identifiers to limit spam and keep access records for administrative actions. For questions about your information, corrections, or deletion, contact the club through the About page.
