### Content and capability gaps

- The October 23 workshop still says time and location are to be announced.
  Organizers should fill those existing fields once confirmed. Do not invent them.
- Subscription requests are recorded, but newsletter delivery, submission
  confirmation emails, and officer email alerts are deliberately not enabled.
  Keep all entry-point wording consistent with a request for future updates;
  do not describe the form itself as broken or promise delivery.
- Rankings trust browser-reported scores (with input validation and quotas).
  This is a documented integrity limitation, not evidence that current scores
  are fraudulent. If competitive rankings matter, validate run evidence on the
  server; otherwise keep the casual scoring model explicit.
